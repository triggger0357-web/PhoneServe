The Power Sovereignty Initiative
​Vision: A world where the surfaces of our machines generate the power they need to operate while cleaning the air they move through.
​The Edge Tech Solution:
Our "Active Coating" is a triple-threat to environmental waste:
​Scrubbing: It breaks down NO_x and smog using sunlight.
​Recycling: It captures wasted engine heat and turns it back into battery power.
​Harvesting: It captures wind resistance and vibrations to maintain a trickle-charge.
​Application: By coating the 100-node PhoneServe pilot, we create a "Self-Powering Mesh" that requires zero external electricity during daylight or high-activity hours.
