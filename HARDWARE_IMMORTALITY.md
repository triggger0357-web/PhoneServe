The Hardware Immortality Protocol
​Vision: Ending the era of "Planned Obsolescence" by creating surfaces that repair themselves.
​The Edge Tech Advantage:
Traditional coatings fail once they are scratched or cracked. Our Nanite-Infused Mesh treats physical damage as a "trigger" for repair.
​Detection: The mesh senses a loss of electrical continuity.
​Reaction: Micro-capsules at the site of the damage burst, releasing fresh polymer and silver-graphene flakes.
​Restoration: Within minutes, the physical seal and the electrical/thermal properties are restored to 95% of their original state.
​Application: This ensures that PhoneServe nodes, building solar paints, and space propulsion coatings can survive the harshest environments for decades without human maintenance.
