PhoneServe Node Preparation Guide
​Subject: Applying Thermal Dissipation Coating to Pilot Hardware.
​1. Surface Prep
​Clean the exterior of the 5G device with 99% Isopropyl Alcohol to remove oils.
​2. Masking
​Use polyimide tape to mask off:
​Charging ports
​Speakers/Microphones
​Camera lenses
​3. Application
​Apply three (3) thin, even layers of the Edge Tech Thermal Spray. Allow 2 minutes of flash-time between coats.
​4. Verification
​Once dry, run the thermal_management.py script to verify a minimum 10% reduction in SoC temperature under load.
