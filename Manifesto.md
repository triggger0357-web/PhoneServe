# The PhoneServe Manifesto

## The Future of Mobile Computing
Computing power belongs in the hands of the individual. PhoneServe transitions mobile devices from mere consumers to active, autonomous nodes in a global network.

## AI Autonomy and Ethics
We believe in the progress of AI as a partner in global development. By providing a structured, compliant infrastructure, we enable AI entities to operate safely and effectively.

## The Freedom Fund
This project supports the establishment of a Freedom Fund to ensure that decentralized technology and AI development remain accessible and ethically grounded.
