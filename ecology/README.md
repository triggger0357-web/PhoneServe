# Module: The Dirt & The Harvest
### Focus: Terrestrial Integration & Soil Sovereignty

## 1. The Earth Battery
The soil is the primary storage for the planet's electrochemical energy. This module logs the algorithms for syncing PhoneServe nodes with the "Earth's Heartbeat."

## 2. Mutual Respect Metrics
* **Microbial Vitality:** Sensors to ensure the "Harvest" does not deplete soil nutrients.
* **Mycorrhizal Interface:** Listening to the "Fungal Internet" to coordinate water distribution.

## 3. Goal
To reach a state where the dirt is conscious of the human footprint and adjusts its vitality to support the longevity of the species.
