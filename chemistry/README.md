# Module: IronSkin Aerosol
### Focus: Atmospheric Healing & Energy Harvesting

## 1. The Chemical Objective
The IronSkin suspension is a self-healing, nanite-infused aerosol designed to stabilize the ozone and harvest ambient electrical potential from the atmosphere.

## 2. Technical Specifications
* **Self-Healing Properties:** Micro-encapsulated polymers that react to structural friction.
* **Energy Harvesting:** Captures ion-flow from moisture and wind friction.
* **Non-Harm Protocol:** Chemically inert to biological life-forms (The "Bee" Test).

## 3. Deployment Logic
The spray is integrated into building exteriors and infrastructure tools, turning the city into a giant, breathing battery.
