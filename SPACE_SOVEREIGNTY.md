The Infinite Reach Initiative
​Vision: Eliminating the need for heavy, explosive chemical fuel tanks in space.
​The Edge Tech Solution:
We treat the entire hull of the satellite as the fuel tank. Our ET-ASF Coating serves three purposes in the vacuum of space:
​Protection: It shields the internal electronics from solar radiation.
​Thermal Control: It manages the extreme temperature swings between sun and shadow.
​Propulsion: When targeted by a high-frequency laser, the coating ablates into a plasma jet, providing precise, controllable thrust for orbital corrections or deep-space travel.
​Edge Tech Knowledgey is building the software to coordinate ground-to-space laser handshakes for automated navigation.
